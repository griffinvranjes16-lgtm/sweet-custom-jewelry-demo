Questions, Answered

Before you visit.

Do you do jewelry repair?

Yes — cleaning and repair for fine jewelry, heirloom jewelry, and diamond jewelry, done in-store. Ring resizing, stone tightening, clasp repair, and more. Some jobs, like a simple resizing, are often ready the same day.

How does custom design work?

It starts with a one-on-one consultation with our goldsmith. You bring the occasion, the story, or even an old piece — we design from concept to creation, by hand, right here in our hometown. No catalog, no middleman.

Can you remake a family heirloom?

Yes — remaking and restyling heirlooms is some of our favorite work. A grandmother’s ring can become an anniversary band; stones from one piece can begin a whole new one. Bring the piece and we’ll show you what’s possible.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome during normal business hours — we love seeing our customers in person. For custom design consultations, booking ahead is recommended so our goldsmith can give you his full attention.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, financing is available — ask in store about current options when you visit or call.

What is permanent jewelry?

Forever bracelets and anklets, welded on in the store — no clasp, no taking it off. It’s a small ceremony and a lasting piece, done in minutes during your visit.
